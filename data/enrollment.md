# Enrollment and Payment

## Price and payment method
The advertised course price is ARS 120,000 (Argentine pesos), payable by bank transfer.

## Enrollment process
People interested in purchasing the course submit their information through the registration form. The course owner then contacts them to provide bank transfer details and complete enrollment.

## Registration fields
- First name.
- Last name.
- Identity document type and number.
- WhatsApp number, used to add the student to the group.
- City or locality of residence.
- Email address.
- Whether the applicant wants information about future courses and news: yes or no.
- How the applicant heard about the course.

The form is intended for people interested in buying the course and being contacted to proceed with payment.
